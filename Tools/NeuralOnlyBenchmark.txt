No-fallback Level 3 experiment at relief depth 0.5 -- 2026-11-12

This is the requested replacement experiment: retain the existing four-probe
descriptor and 10 -> 12 -> 1 network, remove all local marching, and select
binary decision cutoffs on independent calibration data. Weights are unchanged.
The old hybrid is retained as a separate reference. No marching was restored
to make the standalone model's error rate look better.

Setup: RX 7900 XTX, DX12 SM 6.7, source-built O3DE Editor, VueLiveLink,
1280x720, three fixed views, 600 timestamp samples per view, prewarmed methods,
TAA/vsync off. All methods use relief depth 0.5, Level 1 camera intersections,
6 validation iterations / retry cap 4 / tolerance 1, source R32 heights,
validation Gather off and no relief-distance cap. Hybrid confidence is 0.995.
The user's depth-0.3 preset was restored without saving the level. Results
must not be compared directly to the earlier depth-0.3 hybrid benchmark.

At nine samples, neural-only is 16.5% slower than Gather and essentially costs
the same as the hybrid. Removing fallback alone does not beat the inexpensive
march in this case. At 64, neural-only is 50.1% cheaper than Gather in the
forward pass. Its near-constant cost follows the fixed acquisition/MLP budget.
These timings establish measured costs, not separate register, arithmetic or
wave-divergence contributions; those were not individually profiled.

Graphics-queue spans are respectively 3.1972/3.0193/3.0892 ms at 9 and
4.7405/3.6272/3.1078 ms at 64. Spans include inter-pass gaps. This does not
measure CPU frame time or establish a particular FPS improvement.

Quality is not qualified for a production replacement:
At depth 0.5, independent held-out visibility tests find 3,445 errors in
200,000 requests (1.7225%) for neural-only, versus 966 (0.483%) for baseline.
The requests include grazing/back-facing stress cases; query error rates are
not screen-pixel error rates. The oracle is the FP64 bilinear-cell trace in
the existing frozen displacement frame, including its shared analytic cases.
Per-profile counts, denominators and calibration cutoffs are published in
NeuralOnlyEvaluation.json. Saturated descriptors are counted separately in
the full local report; they continue to classify and never start a march.

All six Gather still images match their baseline images pixel for pixel.
Neural-only changes 3.00%-13.20% of pixels across the captured views; 0.13%-2.31%
of pixels differ by more than 8/255 in a channel, with a maximum difference of
27/255. Approximate shadow decisions affect many more pixels than the hybrid.
Fixed-view/motion screenshots are retained locally for visual inspection;
these checks do not prove temporal stability for arbitrary light/camera motion.

A regression test constructs two positions in one height texture with identical
ten-float descriptors but opposite independently checked visibility because a
thin ridge falls between probes. The existing descriptor omits information
needed for that case. A material-specific spatial feature atlas and a small
decoder are the appropriate next representation experiment.

Contract and validation:
The production neural-only forward shader is compiled separately, with neither
local marcher present after preprocessing. Material preparation rejects missing
supported profiles/heights, depths other than calibrated 0.5 and schedules
outside 0 or 5-64. The live Editor checks confirmed depth and schedule rejection.
The 22 standalone groups pass, including 49,152 neural-only CPU/GPU comparisons
per shader model (SM 6.2/6.7), fixed maximum-five-quad checks, binary thresholds,
and nine API/compilation/information-limit cases. Shader processing and the
dedicated Editor capture completed successfully, with no Editor assertions.
All component/camera/CVar restoration checks pass. The saved level is unchanged.
